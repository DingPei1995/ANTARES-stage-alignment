Recomputing ST, SZ, SN after a rotation (SRn, SRz)

ANTARES sample stage — closed-form version of StageModel / relocate() in QuickAlignment (src/quickalignment/core/stage.py, alignment/rotation.py). Verified numerically against the code.

Problem

A point of interest on the sample sits on the analyzer focus at the motor positions \((\ST_0, \SZ_0, \SN_0, \SRn_0, \SRz_0)\). Given new rotations \((\SRn_1, \SRz_1)\), find \((\ST_1, \SZ_1, \SN_1)\) that keep the same sample point on the focus.

Frames and conventions

  • All vectors are written in the stage translation axes, ordered \((\ST, \SN, \SZ)\): \(+\ST \parallel \hat t = (1,-1,0)/\sqrt2\), \(+\SN \parallel \hat n = (1,1,0)/\sqrt2\) (away from the analyzer), \(+\SZ \parallel \hat z\) (up). \((\hat t, \hat n, \hat z)\) is right-handed.
  • Plate frame \((u, v, w)\): at \(\SRn = \SRn_\mathrm{ref}\), \(\SRz = 0\): \(u \parallel +\ST\), \(v \parallel +\SZ\), \(w\) = height above the back face, pointing towards the analyzer (\(-\SN\)).
  • Mechanical stack, bottom to top: translations \(\to\) SRz \(\to\) SRn \(\to\) piezo \(\to\) plate.

Calibration parameters

SymbolMeaningType
\(\ST_c,\ \SZ_c\)ST, SZ putting the plate centre on the focusinitial position
\(\SN_f\)SN putting the reference surface \(w = 5\) mm (standard riser top) in the focal planeinitial position
\(\SRn_\mathrm{ref}\)SRn with the plate vertical, tab up (120°)reference angle
\(\bm c = (c_u, c_v)\)SRn axis position on the plate (plate frame, piezo centred)rotation axis
\(\delta_t,\ \delta_n\)SRz axis offset from the plate-surface centre line, along \(+\ST\) and \(+\SN\)rotation axis
\(\bm\pi\)piezo shift \(\bm\pi = \big(\mathrm{PIX}-150,\ -(\mathrm{PIY}-150)\big)/1000\) mm—

Rotation matrices

\[ \alpha = \SRn - \SRn_\mathrm{ref}, \qquad R(\alpha) = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\alpha & -\sin\alpha \\ \sin\alpha & \cos\alpha \end{pmatrix} \quad\text{(in-plane, }(u,v)\text{)} \] \[ \beta = \SRz, \qquad R_z(\beta) = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\beta & \sin\beta & 0 \\ -\sin\beta & \cos\beta & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \quad\text{(in }(\ST,\SN,\SZ)\text{)} \]

Formula

Step 0 — which plate point is on the focus initial positionsboth axes

Lever arm of the focused point from the SRz axis at the old position, then back to the plate frame:

\[ \bm D_0 = -\begin{pmatrix} \ST_0 - \ST_c + \delta_t \\ \SN_0 - \SN_f + \delta_n \\ \SZ_0 - \SZ_c \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \bm d_0 = R_z(\beta_0)^{\mathsf T}\,\bm D_0 \] \[ \bm q_0 = \begin{pmatrix} d_{0,t} + \delta_t \\ d_{0,z} \end{pmatrix}, \qquad w = 5 - \delta_n - d_{0,n}, \qquad \bm r = R(-\alpha_0)\,(\bm q_0 - \bm c) + \bm c \;\; \big(= (u,v) + \bm\pi\big) \]

Step 1 — SRn rotation about its axis SRn axis

\[ \bm q_1 = R(\alpha_1)\,(\bm r - \bm c) + \bm c \]

Step 2 — lever arm from the SRz axis SRz axis

\[ \bm d_1 = \begin{pmatrix} q_{1,u} - \delta_t \\ (5 - w) - \delta_n \\ q_{1,v} \end{pmatrix} \]

Step 3 — SRz rotation

\[ \bm D_1 = R_z(\beta_1)\,\bm d_1 \]

Step 4 — new translations

\[ \begin{pmatrix} \ST_1 \\ \SN_1 \\ \SZ_1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \ST_0 \\ \SN_0 \\ \SZ_0 \end{pmatrix} - \left(\bm D_1 - \bm D_0\right) \;\;\Longleftrightarrow\;\; \begin{cases} \ST_1 = \ST_c - \delta_t - D_{1,t} \\ \SN_1 = \SN_f - \delta_n - D_{1,n} \\ \SZ_1 = \SZ_c - D_{1,z} \end{cases} \]

Optional: keep SN (usual beamline practice):

\[ \SN_1' = \SN_0, \qquad \ST_1' = \ST_1 + (\SN_1 - \SN_0) \]

The point then slides along the beam by \(\sqrt2\,(\SN_1 - \SN_0)\), and the focusing optic refocuses on it.

Where each correction enters

SRn axis \((c_u, c_v)\)Steps 0, 1 Centre of the in-plane rotation. Matters only when SRn changes: if \(\alpha_1 = \alpha_0\), then \(\bm q_1 = \bm q_0\) and \(\bm c\) drops out.
SRz axis \((\delta_t, \delta_n)\)Steps 0, 2 Lever arm \(\bm d\). Matters only when SRz changes: if \(\beta_1 = \beta_0\), then \(\bm D_1 - \bm D_0 = R_z(\beta)(\bm d_1 - \bm d_0)\) and \(\delta_t, \delta_n\) cancel.

An error in \(\ST_c\) or \(\SZ_c\) therefore acts like an error in the axis position.
